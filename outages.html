Emergency disconnection schedule (GAO)

Status today: ...

The schedule of emergency disconnections is applied only by order of the National Dispatch Centre in case of a shortage of power or water in the system. It is approved every year before the autumn-winter period. When the schedule is in force, each group is disconnected in turn for not more than 2 hours, and the turn is announced on IradOne, on this site and in the running line.
