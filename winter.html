In winter the consumption of electricity in the Zone grows by almost half compared with summer. The highest load is on frosty working days between 18:00 and 22:00. On 14 January 2026 the load reached 1,512 MW, the highest in the history of the company.

The forecast winter maximum for 2026-27 is about 1,560 MW. With the refitted units of Toktogul the company expects to cover it without restrictions. No additional restrictions for consumers are planned for the heating season 2026-27.

Advice for consumers

  • Do not switch on electric heaters, boilers and washing machines at the same time, especially from 18:00 to 22:00.
  • Do not use home-made heaters and do not connect heaters through extension leads.
  • One 2 kW heater for 6 hours a day costs about 115 som a month.
  • If the voltage is low or the lights flicker, call your dispatch service. Do not try to repair the network yourself.
  • Do not approach fallen wires closer than 8 metres. Call 1-55.
