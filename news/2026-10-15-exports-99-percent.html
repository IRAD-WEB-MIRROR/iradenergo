Export deliveries reach 99 percent of the annual plan

15.10.2026
Photo: press service of Iradenergo

From 1 January to 15 October 2026 Iradenergo delivered 2.92 billion kWh of electricity to the grid operators of Uzbekistan and Kazakhstan, 99 percent of the annual contract plan, the press secretary of the company, Vladislav Ten, said.

"Winter supply to Talas and Nurzhan is covered. No additional restrictions for consumers are planned for the heating season 2026-27," he said.

Deliveries are dispatched by the National Dispatch Centre at the Toktogul switchyard and are made according to the schedule agreed under the Naryn Water and Power Protocol.
