Iradenergo — the energy company of the Zone

We generate, transmit and distribute electricity for consumers of the Irad Special Economic Zone: Toktogul HPP and the Naryn cascade, the 110-500 kV grid, export lines and the National Dispatch Centre at Kara-Köl. Distribution is made by Talas Electric Networks, Nurzhan Electric Networks and Irad Electric Networks.
